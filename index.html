About Me

Building on Site, Drafting in BIM, Automating with AI

From pile to handover — and from drawings to software. Here is my story and how I work.

I am a civil engineer from Chattogram, Bangladesh, whose career spans the full life of a building — from pile to handover. I began as a Site Engineer with Seacom Group, supervising multi-storey construction to turnkey delivery, including a 7-storey residential tower and the pile-to-7th-floor scope of a 10-storey school building. I then grew into a Project Engineer at PRONAYON, independently delivering 17+ bank branch interiors and renovations across Bangladesh — from Chattogram to Sylhet, Dhaka, Kushtia and beyond.

Today, as a BIM Engineer at ORTHOTROPIC (Structure Department), I produce AutoCAD and Revit BIM deliverables — structural drafting, plumbing design calculations per IPC/BNBC, estimates and BOQs — for multi-storey towers up to 18 storeys. Alongside engineering, I develop software: a proprietary plumbing calculation tool automating WSFU/DFU fixture-unit and pipe sizing, plus custom scripts and AI agent workflows (Claude, Gemini, Freebuff) that accelerate drafting production, data extraction and office documentation.

To leverage 6+ years of multidisciplinary civil engineering, BIM coordination, and software development expertise to pioneer AI-driven workflow automation; driving organizational efficiency, eliminating repetitive manual tasks, and leading the digital transformation of engineering and corporate offices.
